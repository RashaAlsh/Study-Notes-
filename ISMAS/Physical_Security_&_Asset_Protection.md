Physical Security & Asset Protection Controls

ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A Controls

Overview

Physical security controls protect information assets from physical threats such as unauthorized access, environmental failures, theft, and improper disposal of equipment.

Three important physical security areas include:

* 7.6 Working in Secure Areas
* 7.11 Supporting Utilities
* 7.14 Secure Disposal or Reuse of Equipment

These controls help protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

⸻

7.6 Working in Secure Areas

Overview

Sensitive information may require restricted physical environments where access is carefully controlled.

Examples of sensitive information:

* Customer data
* Human resources (HR) information
* Financial records
* Confidential business information

Secure areas may include:

* Data centers
* Server rooms
* Restricted offices
* Clean rooms

⸻

Security Measures

Organizations should implement additional controls for secure areas.

Restricted Access

* Limit access to authorized personnel only.
* Use security badges, access cards, or biometric controls.
* Regularly review access permissions.

Restrictions on Personal Items

To reduce the risk of information leakage:

* Personal phones may be restricted.
* Notebooks or unauthorized recording devices may be prohibited.
* Cameras may not be allowed.

Information Handling Controls

Rules may include:

* No unauthorized printing.
* No removal of sensitive documents.
* No copying information outside secure areas.

Identification and Enforcement

Secure areas should be:

* Clearly identified.
* Protected by appropriate access controls.
* Governed by documented rules and procedures.

7.11 Supporting Utilities

Overview

Supporting utilities are essential services required for normal operations.

Examples include:

* Electricity
* Air conditioning
* Water supply
* Telecommunications
* Network connectivity

Failure of these services can cause:

* System downtime
* Data loss
* Service interruption
* Equipment damage

⸻

Security Measures

Regular Maintenance and Testing

Organizations should:

* Maintain critical infrastructure.
* Test backup systems regularly.
* Monitor utility performance.

Redundancy

Critical services should have alternatives available.

Examples:

* Backup power systems (UPS/generators)
* Multiple internet connections
* Redundant network equipment
* Alternative cooling systems

Purpose

These measures improve availability and reduce the impact of utility failures.

7.14 Secure Disposal or Reuse of Equipment

Overview

When equipment is disposed of or reused, organizations must ensure that sensitive data cannot be recovered.

Examples of equipment:

* Computers
* Hard drives
* USB storage devices
* Backup media
* Mobile devices

Improper disposal can lead to:

* Data leakage
* Privacy violations
* Regulatory issues
* Security incidents

⸻

Secure Data Removal Methods

Wipe / Erase

Basic deletion of information from storage.

* Removes files from the device.
* May not fully prevent recovery.

Clear

Overwrites storage once to make data recovery more difficult.

Purge

Uses stronger methods to remove data permanently.

Examples:

* Multiple overwrites
* Specialized secure erase tools

Degaussing

Uses magnetic fields to destroy data stored on magnetic media.

Applicable to:

* Traditional hard drives
* Magnetic tapes

Not suitable for all modern storage types.

Destruction

Physically destroys the device or storage media.

Examples:

* Shredding drives
* Crushing storage devices
* Physical damage preventing reuse

Relationship to Information Security

These controls support the three principles of information security:

Principle	Protection Provided
Confidentiality	Prevent unauthorized access and data exposure
Integrity	Prevent unauthorized changes or misuse
Availability	Maintain operational services and infrastructure

⸻

Summary

Physical security controls are essential for protecting information beyond technical safeguards.

* 7.6 Working in Secure Areas protects sensitive information through controlled physical environments.
* 7.11 Supporting Utilities ensures critical services remain available through maintenance and redundancy.
* 7.14 Secure Disposal or Reuse of Equipment prevents data leakage by securely removing information before equipment disposal or reuse.

Together, these controls reduce physical risks and support the overall security objectives of an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
