ISO/IEC# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.5 Organizational Controls –

| Control |  Purpose |
|----------|-----------|
| A.5.1 Information security policies | Set the organization's security vision, expectations, and governance direction. |
| A.5.2 Information security roles and responsibilities | Ensure accountability for security across the business. |
| A.5.3 Segregation of duties | Prevent fraud, abuse, and single points of failure. |
| A.5.4 Management responsibilities | Hold leaders accountable for implementing security within their functions. |
| A.5.5 Contact with authorities | Enable rapid engagement with regulators and authorities when required. |
| A.5.6 Contact with special interest groups | Stay informed about industry developments, threats, and best practices. |
| A.5.7 Threat intelligence | Anticipate emerging threats before they impact the business. |
| A.5.8 Information security in project management | Ensure new initiatives are secure by design. |
| A.5.9 Inventory of information and other associated assets | Know what business assets exist and require protection. |
| A.5.10 Acceptable use of information and other associated assets | Ensure employees use company assets responsibly. |
| A.5.11 Return of assets | Prevent loss of company property and information when personnel leave. |
| A.5.12 Classification of information | Apply protection levels based on business value and sensitivity. |
| A.5.13 Labelling of information | Make security requirements visible and understandable. |
| A.5.14 Information transfer | Protect sensitive information when shared internally or externally. |
| A.5.15 Access control | Ensure only authorized individuals access critical information. |
| A.5.16 Identity management | Maintain control over who can access business resources. |
| A.5.17 Authentication information | Protect credentials that safeguard business systems. |
| A.5.18 Access rights | Ensure access is granted and removed according to business need. |
| A.5.19 Information security in supplier relationships | Reduce risks introduced by third-party suppliers. |
| A.5.20 Addressing information security within supplier agreements | Contractually enforce security expectations with suppliers. |
| A.5.21 Managing information security in the ICT supply chain | Protect the business from supply-chain vulnerabilities. |
| A.5.22 Monitoring, review and change management of supplier services | Ensure suppliers continue to meet security obligations. |
| A.5.23 Information security for use of cloud services | Govern cloud adoption while managing associated risks. |
| A.5.24 Information security incident management planning and preparation | Ensure the organization is prepared for cyber incidents. |
| A.5.25 Assessment and decision on information security events | Enable timely and informed response decisions. |
| A.5.26 Response to information security incidents | Minimize business impact when incidents occur. |
| A.5.27 Learning from information security incidents | Strengthen resilience through continuous improvement. |
| A.5.28 Collection of evidence | Protect the organization's legal and investigative interests. |
| A.5.29 Information security during disruption | Maintain trust and security during crises. |
| A.5.30 ICT readiness for business continuity | Ensure critical services can recover and continue operating. |
| A.5.31 Legal, statutory, regulatory and contractual requirements | Avoid legal penalties and compliance failures. |
| A.5.32 Intellectual property rights | Protect the organization's intellectual assets and reputation. |
| A.5.33 Protection of records | Preserve business records needed for operations, compliance, and evidence. |
| A.5.34 Privacy and protection of PII | Protect customer, employee, and stakeholder personal data. |
| A.5.35 Independent review of information security | Obtain objective assurance that security controls are effective. |
| A.5.36 Compliance with policies, rules and standards for information security | Ensure security requirements are consistently followed. |
| A.5.37 Documented operating procedures | Enable reliable, repeatable, and secure business operations. |

## Executive Summary

The Annex A.5 Organizational Controls help management:

- Protect business-critical information and assets.
- Manage cyber and operational risks.
- Meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.
- Govern suppliers and cloud providers effectively.
- Respond to and recover from incidents and disruptions.
- Maintain customer trust and organizational reputation.
- Demonstrate accountability and effective governance.

**In one sentence:** Annex A.5 ensures the organization has the governance, accountability, risk management, resilience, and compliance framework necessary to protect the business and achieve its objectives.
