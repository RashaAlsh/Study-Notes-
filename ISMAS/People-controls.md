# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.6 People Controls – MD Perspective

| Control | MD Purpose |
|----------|-----------|
| A.6.1 Screening | Reduce the risk of hiring individuals who could pose a security, fraud, or compliance risk. |
| A.6.2 Terms and conditions of employment | Ensure employees understand their security responsibilities from the outset. |
| A.6.3 Information security awareness, education and training | Build a security-conscious workforce and reduce human-related risks. |
| A.6.4 Disciplinary process | Provide a consistent approach for addressing security policy violations. |
| A.6.5 Responsibilities after termination or change of employment | Protect business information when employees leave or change roles. |
| A.6.6 Confidentiality or non-disclosure agreements | Safeguard sensitive information through legally enforceable commitments. |
| A.6.7 Remote working | Manage security risks arising from employees working outside company premises. |
| A.6.8 Information security event reporting | Encourage timely reporting of security issues to minimize business impact. |

## Executive Summary

The Annex A.6 People Controls help management:

- Hire trustworthy personnel.
- Clearly define employee security responsibilities.
- Build security awareness and competence.
- Protect information throughout the employee lifecycle.
- Manage remote-working risks.
- Encourage rapid reporting of security concerns.
- Reduce insider threats and human error.

**In one sentence:** Annex A.6 ensures that people understand, accept, and fulfill their information security responsibilities throughout their relationship with the organization.
