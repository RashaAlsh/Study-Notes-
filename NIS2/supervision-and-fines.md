## 1. Supervision: Proactive vs. Reactive
* **Essential Entities:** Regulated via **Proactive (Ex-ante)** supervision. The authority conducts regular audits, targeted inspections, and random checks.
* **Important Entities:** Regulated via **Reactive (Ex-post)** supervision. Action is taken only if there is evidence or an indication of non-compliance (e.g., an incident or complaint).

---

## 2. Enforcement Powers and Maximum Fines
Enforcement happens in two stages: binding instructions/warnings first, followed by administrative penalties.

| Metric | Essential Entities | Important Entities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum Financial Fine** | Up to **€10 Million** or **2%** of global annual turnover *(whichever is higher)* | Up to **€7 Million** or **1.4%** of global annual turnover *(whichever is higher)* |
| **Supervisory Nature** | Proactive & Regular Audits | Reactive (Ex-post) |

---

## 3. Ultimate Enforcement Actions (Non-Public Entities)
If initial enforcement instructions fail, competent authorities can execute advanced powers:
1. **Certification Suspension:** Temporarily halting the entity's right to provide services.
2. **Managerial Prohibition:** Temporarily banning any natural person at the **CEO or legal representative level** from exercising managerial functions.
3. **Personal Liability:** Corporate executives can be held **personally liable** for breaches of their duty to ensure compliance.
