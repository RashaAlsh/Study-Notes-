
 NIS2 
 Study Notes repository! This is a curated collection of deep-dives, cheat sheets, and study summaries focused on the EU Network and Information Security (NIS2) Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555).
This repository is designed to help understand the legal requirements.

1. Who falls under the scope?
As a rule of thumb, NIS2 applies to medium and large enterprises (more than 50 employees OR over €10M annual turnover) operating within the designated sectors.

| Entity Classification | Essential Entities (Annex I) | Important Entities (Annex II) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical Sectors | Energy, Transport, Banking, Health, Water, Digital Infrastructure | Postal, Waste Management, Chemicals, Food, Manufacturing, Research, Digital Providers |
| Supervision | Ex-ante & Ex-post: Subject to proactive audits and inspections at any time. | Ex-post only: Subject to audits only after evidence of an incident or non-compliance. |
| Fines (Max) | Up to €10 Million or 2% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher). | Up to €7 Million or 1.4% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher). |
2. The "10 Minimum Measures" (Article 21)
All in-scope entities are required to implement these baseline security policies and technical controls:
 * Risk analysis and information system security policies
 * Incident handling processes
 * Business continuity planning (backups, disaster recovery, crisis management)
 * Supply chain security (managing risk across all third-party suppliers)
 * Security in acquisition, development, and maintenance of networks/systems (vulnerability handling)
 * Policies to assess the effectiveness of cybersecurity risk-management measures
 * Basic cyber hygiene practices and cybersecurity training
 * Cryptography and encryption policies
 * Human resources security, access control, and asset management
 * Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and secure communication systems
3. The Strict Reporting Timeline
One of the toughest operational changes under NIS2 is the multi-stage incident reporting requirement for "significant incidents":
   ⚠️ Incident Occurs 
          │
          ▼ [Within 24 Hours]
   🚨 1. Early Warning (Warning on suspected impact)
          │
          ▼ [Within 72 Hours]
   📝 2. Incident Notification (Assessment, severity details)
          │
          ▼ [Within 1 Month]
   🏁 3. Final Report (Root cause, mitigation measures taken)
