##  1. Top Management Governance (Article roles-and-standards.md 20)
* Management (CEOs/Boards) must directly approve and oversee cybersecurity measures.
* **Mandatory Training:** Executives are legally required to undergo cybersecurity risk training.
* Security should be placed on the regular **board agenda**.

## 2. The Strategic CISO Role
* A full-time role is **not** explicitly required (external/outsourced security expertise is permitted), but clear coordination control must be demonstrated.
* Key focuses: Strategy development, risk management programs, vulnerability handling, and supply chain control.

## ⚖️ 3. CISO vs. DPO Alignment
* **CISO Focus:** Technical and organizational resilience of networks.
* **DPO Focus:** Protecting the rights of natural persons regarding data privacy (GDPR).
* **Reporting Duality:** A single incident might require a 24-hour NIS2 report (via CISO) and a 72-hour GDPR report (via DPO). Member States are required to simplify this via **single-entry points**.

## ⛓️ 4. Supply Chain Risk & Employee Factors
* **Third-Party Liability:** If a critical supplier falls outside the NIS2 scope, **your organization remains legally liable** for their compliance state. Ensure security requirements are bound in supplier agreements.
* **Human Psychology:** Threat actors exploit human behavior via phishing. Organizations must leverage simplified incident notification tools for employees and implement continuous awareness programs.

---

## 5. Alignment with International Standards (ISO)
NIS2 promotes the use of international standards (Article 25) to avoid duplication of effort:

* **ISO/IEC 27001 (ISMS):** Excellent mapping for the technical, operational, and organizational controls demanded by NIS2 Article 21.
*  **The Business Continuity Gap:** ISO 27001 Annex A is insufficient for NIS2's full business continuity and crisis management mandates (Article 21(2c)).
* **The Solution:** Organizations should adopt **EN-ISO 22301 (BCMS)** alongside ISO 27001 to fully cover backup management, disaster recovery, and crisis resilience.
