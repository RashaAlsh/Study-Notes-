# Scope and Entity Classification

## 1. Enterprise Size Definitions (2003/361/EC)
NIS2 utilizes specific financial and staff thresholds to categorize organizations:

| Enterprise Category | Staff Headcount | Annual Turnover | Balance Sheet Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Medium-sized** | < 250 persons | ≤ €50 Million | OR ≤ €43 Million |
| **Small** | < 50 persons | ≤ €10 Million | AND/OR ≤ €10 Million |
| **Micro** | < 10 persons | ≤ €2 Million | AND/OR ≤ €2 Million |

## 2. CER vs. NIS2 Entity Alignment
There is a mandatory regulatory link between the **CER Directive** (physical resilience) and **NIS2 Directive** (cybersecurity):

>  **The Golden Rule:** Any entity identified as a **Critical Entity** under CER automatically qualifies as an **Essential Entity** under NIS2 (Article 3(1f)).

### CER Selection Criteria:
1. Provides one or more essential services.
2. Operates, and its critical infrastructure is located, within the EU.
3. An incident would cause **significant disruptive effects**.

 **Timeline Conflict:** Member States must establish the NIS2 entity list by **17 April 2025**, whereas the CER critical entity identification deadline is **17 July 2026**.

---

## 3. NIS2 Essential Entities Classification Rules
Entities are automatically classified as **Essential** if:
* They are listed in **Annex I** and exceed the medium-sized enterprise thresholds (Large Entities).
* They are qualified trust service providers, top-level domain name registries, or DNS service providers, **regardless of their size**.
