## Governance Goal

Manage outsourcing risk while ensuring:

- Compliance

- Security

- Operational resilience

- Business continuity

---

## Lifecycle Process

1. **Source / Plan**

   - Define requirements

   - Determine risk tier

2. **Evaluate**

   - Perform due diligence

   - Conduct risk assessments

3. **Contract**

   - Add security clauses

   - Define SLAs and audit rights

4. **Manage**

   - Onboard vendor securely

   - Apply technical controls

5. **Monitor**

   - Track KRIs

   - Conduct ongoing audits

6. **Exit**

   - Revoke access

   - Sanitize and recover data
