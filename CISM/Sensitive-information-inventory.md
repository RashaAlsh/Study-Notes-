# CISM – Sensitive Information Inventory

## Definition
A sensitive information inventory is a **comprehensive list of all sensitive data assets** within an organization, including where they are stored, processed, and transmitted.

---

## Purpose
- Identify and classify **sensitive data**
- Support **risk management**
- Enable proper **security controls**
- Meet **legal and regulatory requirements**

---

## What is Included

### Data Identification
- What data exists (PII, financial, health, intellectual property)

### Data Classification
- Sensitivity levels (e.g., Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted)

### Data Location
- Where data is stored (databases, cloud, endpoints)

### Data Ownership
- Assigned **Data Owner**

### Data Flow
- How data is transmitted and processed

### Retention Requirements
- How long data must be kept

---

## Key Components

- **Data Type** (PII, financial, etc.)
- **Classification Level**
- **Storage Location**
- **Owner**
- **Access Rights**
- **Protection Requirements**

---

## Roles Involved

- **Data Owner** → Classifies and approves access  
- **Data Steward** → Ensures data quality and proper use  
- **Data Custodian** → Implements security controls  

---

## Benefits

- Improves **visibility of sensitive data**
- Reduces risk of **data breaches**
- Supports **compliance (e.g., GDPR)**
- Enables effective **access control**

---

## Key Concepts

- You cannot protect what you **don’t know exists**
- Inventory is the **foundation of data protection**
- Must be **regularly updated**

---

## Exam Tips

- Inventory comes **before classification and protection**
- Focus on **data-centric security**, not just systems
- Always link to **risk management and business impact**

---

## Quick Memory Trick

> Identify → Classify → Locate → Protect → Monitor
