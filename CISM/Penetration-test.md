# CISM Operations: Penetration Testing Types

## 1. Knowledge-Based Profiles
*   **Black-Box:** Zero prior knowledge. Focuses on external perimeter defense.
*   **Gray-Box:** Limited knowledge (e.g., standard user login). Focuses on lateral movement and privilege escalation.
*   **White-Box:** Full knowledge (source code/configs). Focuses on deep code logic and architectural flaws.

## 2. Special Testing Strategies
*   **Red Teaming:** A stealthy, goal-oriented exercise simulating an adversarial attack (e.g., "steal the HR database") without alerting the internal IT team. Tests the organization's **detection and response** times.
*   **Blue Team:** The internal security team defending the environment.
*   **Purple Teaming:** A collaborative exercise where the Red Team attacks while the Blue Team watches in real-time, optimizing defensive controls (IDS/SIEM) on the fly.

## 3. Governance Constraints
*   **Rules of Engagement (RoE):** A mandatory document defining boundaries, allowed IP addresses, testing hours, and what targets are strictly off-limits (e.g., production databases).
