# CISM Frameworks: NIST Summary

## 1. NIST SP 800-53 (The Library)
* **Definition:** A massive catalog of security and privacy controls for information systems.
* **CISM Use:** Used as the technical "menu" when a Data Custodian needs to implement specific protections (e.g., AC-2 for Account Management).

## 2. NIST SP 800-61 (Incident Response)
* **The Lifecycle:**
    1. Preparation
    2. Detection & Analysis
    3. Containment, Eradication, & Recovery
    4. Post-Incident Activity (Lessons Learned)

## 3. NIST SP 800-30 (Risk Assessment)
* **Goal:** Provides guidance for conducting risk assessments of federal information systems and organizations.
* **Process:** Identify Threat Sources -> Identify Vulnerabilities -> Determine Likelihood -> Determine Impact -> Calculate Risk.

## 4. NIST SP 800-34 (Contingency Planning)
* **Focus:** Detailed instructions for Business Continuity (BCP) and Disaster Recovery (DRP).
