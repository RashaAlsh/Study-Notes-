# CISM Third-Party Management: Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Definition
A formal agreement between a service provider and a client that defines the expected level of service, the metrics by which that service is measured, and the remedies or penalties should the service levels not be achieved.

## 2. Critical Security Components
* **Incident Response Times:** Tiered response windows based on severity (e.g., Critical = 1 hour, Low = 24 hours).
* **System Availability:** Guaranteed uptime percentages supporting the Business Impact Analysis (BIA).
* **RPO & RTO:** Specific targets for Data Recovery Point (data loss) and Recovery Time (downtime).
* **Change Management:** Notification windows before the provider performs maintenance.

## 3. Remedies and Penalties
* **Service Credits:** The most common remedy; the vendor provides a discount on the next month's bill.
* **Termination Rights:** If the SLA is "materially breached" (e.g., three consecutive months of failures), the client can exit the contract without penalty.
